About QuakeEscape

QuakeEscape is a capstone project developed by a dedicated team of students passionate about public safety education. Our 2D adventure game transforms traditional earthquake preparedness training into an engaging, interactive experience.

Through immersive storytelling and realistic scenarios, players learn critical survival skills that could save lives during real earthquakes. Every level is designed with input from emergency professionals to ensure accuracy and effectiveness.

Special Thanks: We extend our deepest gratitude to Central 911 for their invaluable partnership, expert guidance, and commitment to validating our educational content.

The 3 Critical Phases

Master earthquake safety with QuakeEscape's comprehensive three-phase educational approach

01

Before the Earthquake

  • Secure heavy furniture and appliances
  • Create emergency supply kits
  • Develop family communication plans
  • Identify safe spots in each room
  • Practice "Drop, Cover, and Hold On"
02

During the Earthquake

  • Drop to your hands and knees immediately
  • Cover your head and neck under shelter
  • Hold on to your shelter until shaking stops
  • Stay away from windows and glass
  • Do not run outside during shaking
03

After the Earthquake

  • Check yourself and others for injuries
  • Expect aftershocks - be ready
  • Inspect your home for damage
  • Listen to emergency broadcasts
  • Use phones only for emergencies

How to Play

Get started with your earthquake preparedness adventure in four simple steps

1

Launch the 2D Adventure

Start QuakeEscape and enter our immersive 2D world. Choose between "Story Mode" for guided learning or "Challenge Mode" to test your skills in realistic scenarios.

2

Navigate Interactive Scenarios

Control your character through realistic earthquake situations. Make decisions about where to take cover, what supplies to gather, and how to respond to various hazards.

3

Complete Knowledge Checks

After each level, test your understanding with interactive quizzes. Get instant feedback and learn why certain actions save lives during real earthquakes.
